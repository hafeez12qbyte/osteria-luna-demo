Our Story

From a village kitchen in Emilia-Romagna to Taylor Street

Osteria Luna began in 2009, when the Morelli family brought their recipes from Modena to Chicago's Little Italy — the same ragù that simmered on their grandmother's stove, the same hand-rolled tagliatelle.

Nothing here is rushed. Dough rests overnight. Sauces take the day they take. And the menu stays short on purpose: twelve dishes, done properly, changing with what the market brings each morning.

Come hungry. Leave family.

— The Morelli Family
